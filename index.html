LiftDay

A strength training log for iPhone that plans your next workout from what you actually lifted.

Build a template by hand, from a short form, or with Apple Intelligence. Log your sets at the gym, and LiftDay works out your next weight and reps, suggests changes when you stall, and writes a short weekly review.

Your workouts stay on your iPhone. No account, no sign-in.
