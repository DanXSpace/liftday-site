LiftDay Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

LiftDay is a strength training log for iPhone. In short: your workouts and your body weight stay on your phone. LiftDay has no accounts and no servers of its own. The only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous counts of which features get used, and you can turn those off.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into LiftDay is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your templates, exercises and starting weights
  • your workouts: every set, weight and rep count
  • suggestions, weekly reviews and your settings

LiftDay doesn't upload any of this, and there's no LiftDay account to sync it to. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes LiftDay's data, the same as other apps. Apple handles those backups, and you control them in your iPhone's settings.

The AI features

LiftDay's AI features, building a template from a description and writing your weekly review, use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. What you type and your training numbers are processed on the device. They're never sent to LiftDay, to Apple or to anyone else for these features. On an iPhone without Apple Intelligence, you can build a template from a short form instead.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and you choose what LiftDay may access in the Health permission screen. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone's Settings.

If you allow it, LiftDay:

  • reads your body weight to show it next to your training
  • reads your other workouts, only to check whether your Apple Watch already recorded the same session, so it's never saved twice
  • saves each finished session to Apple Health as a workout (its type, start and end time), and deletes it from Health if you delete the session in LiftDay
  • saves weigh-ins you add in LiftDay, and deletes them from Health if you delete them in LiftDay.

Data from Apple Health is only ever used on your phone. LiftDay never sends it anywhere, never uses it for analytics or advertising, and never stores it in iCloud.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features help people and where they get stuck, LiftDay sends anonymous counts to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built for privacy. Each count is the name of something that happened, for example "a workout was finished" or "a weekly review was opened", sometimes with one fixed word such as "planned" or "kilograms".

These counts never include:

  • any weight, rep count or body weight
  • the names of your exercises or templates
  • anything you type
  • anything from Apple Health.

Each count also carries the app's version number and an anonymous ID. LiftDay makes this ID on your phone as a random value tied to nothing else, sends only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, and replaces it every 90 days. It lets the counts tell a returning person from a new one without knowing who anyone is. LiftDay doesn't use an advertising identifier, your device model, your location or any account. These counts aren't linked to you and aren't used to track you across apps or websites.

To turn them off, open LiftDay, tap the gear on Today, and switch off Share anonymous usage data under Privacy. Once it's off, nothing is sent and the anonymous ID is deleted.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send LiftDay's developer crash reports and performance data. Apple collects these and anonymizes them, and you control them there. LiftDay doesn't use any other crash reporting service.

Sending feedback

If you use Send Feedback in Settings, your Mail app opens a message to LiftDay's support address. Below your message, it adds the LiftDay version, the iOS version, your iPhone model, your weight unit and whether Apple Intelligence is available, to help with fixing problems. You can read and edit all of it before you send. Emails are used only to reply to you and improve LiftDay.

What LiftDay doesn't do

  • no accounts or sign-in
  • no ads and no advertising identifiers
  • no selling or sharing of your data
  • no tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

LiftDay isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Your data lives on your phone, so deleting LiftDay deletes it. Workouts and weigh-ins that LiftDay saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there. The anonymous usage counts can't be traced back to you, so there's nothing in them to delete.
